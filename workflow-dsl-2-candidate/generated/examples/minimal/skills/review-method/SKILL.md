# Review Method

1. Read only the frozen candidate and the raw evidence shared with your lens.
2. Derive counterexamples from the declared Goal/Interface/Project Context.
3. Admit a finding only when it has exact location, applicable authority, evidence, impact and a closure condition.
4. Do not inspect the other lens's analysis before the barrier; your session is isolated.
5. Never vote, never modify the candidate, and never choose a successor.
